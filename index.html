Safe internal temperatures

FoodSafe temp
Poultry (all)165°F / 74°C
Ground meats160°F / 71°C
Beef, pork, lamb (cuts)145°F / 63°C + rest
Fish & shellfish145°F / 63°C

USDA minimums. An instant-read thermometer is the only reliable check — color and timing are not.
