Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to information when you visit Crisp Charts.

Short version: we do not ask you to register, we do not sell anything directly, and we do not build profiles of you. The data that exists comes from two places — Google, which hosts this site, and the advertising partners that pay for it.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Crisp Charts is a content website about cooking times, temperatures and kitchen reference charts, published on the Blogger platform. For any privacy question, write to contact@crispcharts.me.

2. Information you give us

We only receive information you choose to send:

  • If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We keep it only as long as needed to answer you.
  • If you comment, your comment and display name are stored by Blogger under Google's policies.

We do not operate a newsletter, an account system, or a checkout.

3. Information collected automatically

By Google (the platform). This site is served by Blogger, a Google service. Google may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL and timestamps, and may set cookies for its own purposes. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

By our advertising partners. We display advertising supplied by third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies, web beacons, or device identifiers to measure advertising performance and to show ads that are more relevant to you. We do not control those cookies and we do not receive the personal data behind them — we see aggregate reports only.

Third-party vendors, including advertising partners, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising from many providers through aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu where applicable.

What we do not do: we do not currently run our own analytics service, we do not sell personal data, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

4. Cookies

Cookies on this site come from Google and from advertising partners. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will keep working. Blocking cookies does not stop ads from appearing — it may only make them less relevant to you.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to personalised advertising. Because we hold almost no personal data ourselves, requests usually belong with the party that collected it:

  • Data held by Google / Blogger: use Google's own privacy controls.
  • Data held by an advertising partner: contact that network directly, or use the opt-out links in section 3.
  • Anything you sent us by email: write to contact@crispcharts.me and we will delete it.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you may request disclosure of categories of information collected; we do not sell personal information.

6. Legal bases and retention

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for platform and advertising cookies is consent where required, and legitimate interest for security and basic operation. Technical logs are retained by the parties that create them, under their own retention schedules.

7. External links

Articles may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices — read their policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
