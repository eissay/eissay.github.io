contact@crispcharts.me

We usually reply within a few business days.

What to include

  • A correction: the chart, the food, your appliance model, and what actually happened. This is genuinely useful — model differences are the single biggest source of bad times.
  • A question: be specific about your appliance and the food.
  • A licensing or reuse request: tell us what you want to use and where.

What we cannot help with

  • Medical, dietary, or allergy advice. Talk to a qualified professional.
  • Appliance repairs or safety faults. Contact the manufacturer.
  • Guaranteeing a result. We cannot see your kitchen, your oven's real temperature, or your ingredients.
