2. Cooking times are starting points, not instructions

This is the most important warning on the site.

Air fryers, ovens and slow cookers vary widely in wattage, real chamber temperature, basket size, and how close the heating element sits to the food. Two machines set to the same number can finish the same food minutes apart. Ingredient size, starting temperature (frozen, chilled, room temperature), altitude and how full the basket is all change the result too.

Therefore: every time on this site is a tested starting range, and the only reliable way to know that food is safe and done is an instant-read thermometer checked against published safe minimum internal temperatures. When a chart on this site and a thermometer disagree, the thermometer wins, always.

3. Food safety and allergens

  • Cook poultry, stuffed items, ground meat, and reheated leftovers to their safe minimum internal temperature.
  • If you are cooking for anyone with a food allergy or intolerance, verify every ingredient yourself. Recipes and charts cannot account for cross-contamination, brand differences, or reformulations.
  • If you are pregnant, immunocompromised, cooking for young children or older adults, or managing a medical condition, follow the guidance of a qualified professional rather than a general chart.

4. Appliance safety

Follow your manufacturer's instructions on liners, foil, parchment, oil quantity, and safe operating limits. Never use a liner that blocks airflow, and never leave a cooking appliance unattended. Any modification of a recipe or chart to fit your appliance is your own responsibility.
