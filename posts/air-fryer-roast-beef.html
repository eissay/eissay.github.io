Air Fryer Roast Beef: Time and Temperature by Weight

Air fryer roast beef times for small cuts by weight, with the 145°F / 63°C rule, carryover cooking and the rest that keeps it tender.

A small roast beef fits an air fryer basket and comes out with a browned crust and a tender center in under an hour — no oven, no splatter, no watching a big roast dry out. Cuts like eye of round, top round and sirloin tip all work; ribeye and tenderloin roasts work too but cost more than the method needs.

The temperatures are lower than most people expect, and the rest does more work than the basket does.

Times vary by appliance model — use a thermometer. Weight, shape and starting temperature change the clock, but 145°F / 63°C plus rest does not change.

The rule for roast beef#

Roast beef is a whole cut, so the simple USDA standard applies:

CutSafe internal temperatureRest
Beef roast, any whole cut145°F / 63°C3 minutes
Ground beef (not roast beef)160°F / 71°CNone

Probe the thickest part of the roast. Lower internal temperatures with long holding times exist in professional pasteurization charts, but for home cooking the simple rule is the right one: 145°F / 63°C, then rest.

Air fryer roast beef chart#

Times assume a preheated basket and a roast that starts at room temperature — take it out of the refrigerator 30–45 minutes before cooking. A cold center lags and the outside overcooks while it catches up.

RoastTemperatureTimeNotes
1.5–2 lb, eye of round375°F / 190°C30–40 minCheck at 25 min
2–3 lb, top round375°F / 190°C40–50 minTurn at halfway
1–1.5 lb, sirloin tip375°F / 190°C25–35 minProbe the center
2 lb, ribeye roast375°F / 190°C30–40 minFat cap renders; watch drips

Sear-level heat is unnecessary in the air fryer — the moving air browns the surface on its own. Roast at a moderate setting so the center catches up before the crust hardens.

The rest is not optional#

The USDA's 145°F / 63°C standard includes a three minute rest, and in practice the roast keeps cooking after it leaves the basket:

Pull from basketAfter a 10-minute restResult
145°F / 63°CAbout 150°F / 66°CBlush pink center, juicy
150°F / 66°CAbout 155°F / 68°CBarely pink, firm
155°F / 68°CAbout 160°F / 71°CWell-done, noticeably drier

All three are safe — 145°F / 63°C with rest is the floor, and anything above it is texture. Rest the roast 10 minutes under loose foil on a board, then slice thin across the grain.

Slicing and serving#

Slice roast beef thin, across the grain, with a sharp knife — thick slices chew, thin slices melt. Leftover roast beef keeps four days refrigerated and reheats best at low heat:

  • Reheat slices at 320°F / 160°C for 3–5 minutes with a spoon of broth over them.
  • Dry reheating at higher heat turns the slices tough and gray.

Five mistakes with air fryer roast beef#

  1. Cooking from the refrigerator. A cold center lags; rest the roast 30–45 minutes first.
  2. Skipping the thermometer. Color and timing are guesses; the thickest part decides.
  3. Cooking past 145°F / 63°C in the basket. The rest finishes the job more gently than extra heat.
  4. Slicing thick or with the grain. Thin slices across the grain are the difference between tender and chewy.
  5. Roasting at 400°F / 205°C. The crust hardens before the center catches up on anything thicker than an inch.

Frequently asked questions#

How long does roast beef take in an air fryer?

30–40 minutes at 375°F / 190°C for a 1.5–2 pound roast, 40–50 minutes for 2–3 pounds. Start checking 10 minutes before the low end — the roast climbs quickly.

What temperature should roast beef be inside?

145°F / 63°C in the thickest part, followed by a three minute rest, per USDA rules for whole cuts of beef. It stays blush pink — that is correct and safe.

Is pink roast beef safe?

At 145°F / 63°C with a proper rest, yes — that is exactly the USDA guidance for whole cuts. Ground beef is the exception: it must reach 160°F / 71°C.

Can I cook a frozen roast?

Thaw it first — a frozen roast browns long before the center thaws, which makes an accurate reading impossible. A 2–3 pound roast needs a day or more in the refrigerator.

Why is my roast beef gray and dry?

It cooked past temperature or without a rest. Pull at 145°F / 63°C, rest 10 minutes, and slice thin across the grain.

Roast beef in the air fryer comes down to three things: room-temperature start, a verified 145°F / 63°C, and a real rest. Everything else is seasoning.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
