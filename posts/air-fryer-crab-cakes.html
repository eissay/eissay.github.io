Air Fryer Crab Cakes: Time and Temperature for Golden Cakes

Air fryer crab cake times for fresh and frozen cakes, with 400°F / 205°C settings, the 145°F / 63°C internal rule and the chilling rule.

Crab cakes are the appetizer the air fryer handles with the least effort: no pan of oil, no flipping fragile cakes in a skillet — the moving air browns both sides while the cake holds its shape on the basket.

The two rules that matter: chill the cakes before cooking so they hold, and verify the internal temperature of the crab.

Times vary by appliance model. Cake size, filler amount and crowding change the clock, but 145°F / 63°C does not change.

The rule for crab#

Crab is shellfish, and shellfish under FDA guidance cooks to the same standard as fish:

Crab cakeSafe internal temperatureRest
Fresh crab cakes145°F / 63°C—
Frozen crab cakes, cooked from frozen145°F / 63°C—
Leftover crab cakes, reheated145°F / 63°C—

Probe the center of the thickest cake. Crab cakes are delicate — probe from the side at an angle so the cake does not split.

Air fryer crab cake chart#

Chill the shaped cakes at least 30 minutes before cooking — a warm cake sags in the basket. A light spray of oil on both sides gives the golden crust.

CakeTemperatureTimeNotes
Fresh, 3 oz cakes400°F / 205°C8–10 minFlip once, carefully
Fresh, 4 oz cakes400°F / 205°C10–12 minCheck at 9 min
Frozen, store-bought400°F / 205°C10–13 minNo thawing, single layer

The flip is the one delicate moment: a wide, thin spatula and one confident turn at halfway. The cakes firm as they cook, so the second half of the cook is safe to move.

The mix: filler and binder#

A good crab cake is mostly crab — the filler holds, the crab stars. The standard ratio is one egg and a quarter cup of breadcrumbs per pound of crab, with mayonnaise, mustard, Worcestershire and Old Bay seasoning.

ElementAmount per pound of crabWhy
Egg1The binder that sets in the heat
Breadcrumbs1/4 cupHolds moisture and shape
Mayonnaise2 tbspRichness that keeps the cake tender
Old Bay1 tspThe signature seasoning

Too much filler turns the cake into a breadcrumb patty; too little and it falls apart. The ratio above is the balance that holds together and still tastes of crab.

Five mistakes with air fryer crab cakes#

  1. Cooking warm, freshly mixed cakes. They sag in the basket; chill 30+ minutes first.
  2. Flipping with a thin, quick wrist. A wide spatula and one confident turn at halfway.
  3. Overcooking past 145°F / 63°C. Crab turns rubbery fast; pull at temperature.
  4. Too much filler. One egg and a quarter cup of crumbs per pound — the crab should star.
  5. Overcrowding. Cakes touching steam each other; one layer with air between.

Frequently asked questions#

How long do crab cakes take in an air fryer?

8–10 minutes at 400°F / 205°C for 3-ounce cakes, flipped once at halfway. Frozen ones need 10–13 minutes; mini cakes 6–8 minutes.

What temperature should crab cakes be inside?

145°F / 63°C in the center — the FDA standard for shellfish, the same as fish.

Do I need to thaw frozen crab cakes?

No — straight into the basket at 400°F / 205°C for 10–13 minutes. Verify the center of the thickest one.

Why did my crab cakes fall apart?

They were warm from the mix or had too little binder. Chill the shaped cakes 30+ minutes, and keep the ratio at one egg plus a quarter cup of crumbs per pound.

How do I reheat crab cakes?

350°F / 177°C for 5–7 minutes keeps them tender; higher heat turns the crab rubbery. The reheated center should reach 145°F / 63°C.

Crab cakes reward chilling and restraint: 30 minutes cold, one careful flip, pulled at 145°F / 63°C. The air fryer gives the golden crust of pan-frying with none of the oil.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
