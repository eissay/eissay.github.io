Air Fryer Hamburgers: Juicy Patties at the Right Temperature

Air fryer hamburger times for fresh and frozen patties, with 380°F / 193°C settings, flip timing and the 160°F / 71°C ground beef rule.

The air fryer cooks burgers with the same hot air that crisps fries, so the outside browns and the fat that renders stays with the patty instead of dripping onto coals. It is faster than a grill to heat up and less messy than a skillet — but ground beef has a fixed safety rule, and a burger is the one shape where undercooking is easy to miss.

The chart below covers fresh and frozen patties. Under USDA rules, ground beef must reach 160°F / 71°C inside — there is no thickness, cheese or bun that changes that number.

Times vary by appliance model — use a thermometer. Wattage, basket size and patty thickness change the clock, but 160°F / 71°C does not change.

The rule for ground beef#

Whole steaks can be safely cooked to lower temperatures because bacteria live on the surface. Ground beef mixes the surface into the center, so the center must be cooked too:

Ground beefSafe internal temperature
Fresh patties, any thickness160°F / 71°C
Frozen patties, cooked from frozen160°F / 71°C
Burgers reheated later160°F / 71°C

Probe the center of the patty from the side, horizontally, so the thermometer tip sits in the middle of the meat. A vertical probe can push the tip through the thin bottom and read the hot basket air instead.

Air fryer hamburger chart: fresh patties#

Times assume a preheated basket, patties of even thickness and a single layer. Dimple the center of each patty with your thumb — it keeps the middle from bulging.

PattyTemperatureTimeNotes
Fresh, 1/4 lb, 1/2 inch thick380°F / 193°C8–10 minFlip at halfway
Fresh, 1/3 lb, 3/4 inch thick380°F / 193°C10–12 minFlip at halfway
Fresh, 1/2 lb, 1 inch thick380°F / 193°C12–15 minFlip once, check early
Stuffed or filled patty380°F / 193°C12–15 minVerify the center carefully
Smashed-style thin patties380°F / 193°C6–8 minVery thin; probe carefully

Cheese goes on in the last minute, after the patty has reached temperature. The residual heat and 30 seconds in the warm basket melt it without extending the cook.

Air fryer hamburger chart: frozen patties#

Frozen patties are pre-formed and often pre-seasoned, and they cook evenly from frozen. No thawing and no pressing — pressing squeezes moisture out.

Frozen pattyTemperatureTimeNotes
1/4 lb frozen patty380°F / 193°C12–15 minFlip at halfway
1/3 lb frozen patty380°F / 193°C14–17 minFlip at halfway
1/2 lb frozen patty380°F / 193°C16–19 minCheck at 14 minutes
Thin frozen patties380°F / 193°C10–12 minShake or flip once

Frozen patties frequently need the long end of their range. The center thaws as it cooks, so the thermometer should confirm the middle before you build the burger.

Fat, smoke and the drip tray#

Burgers release fat as they cook, and in an air fryer that fat collects under the basket. Two habits keep the smoke down: cook within the chart's temperatures rather than hotter, and empty or wipe the drawer between batches. Adding water to the drip tray — a tablespoon under the basket — also catches spatter and reduces smoke on fatty blends.

An 80/20 blend gives the juiciest result. Leaner beef dries out faster than the chart's shortest time; fattier blends splatter more. Either way, the internal temperature target does not move.

Five mistakes with air fryer burgers#

  1. Judging by the crust. A deep-brown outside can hide a raw center; probe the middle every time.
  2. Pressing patties during cooking. Pressing squeezes out the juices that make burgers tender.
  3. Cooking straight from the fridge. Cold centers lag; let patties sit out 15 minutes for even timing.
  4. Overworking the meat. Compact patties turn dense; mix just until the seasoning is spread.
  5. Skipping the dimple. Flat-center patties dome up and cook unevenly at the edges.

Frequently asked questions#

How long do burgers take in an air fryer?

8–12 minutes at 380°F / 193°C for fresh quarter- to third-pound patties, flipped halfway; frozen patties need 12–17 minutes. Verify the center reaches 160°F / 71°C.

What temperature do air fryer burgers need inside?

160°F / 71°C — the USDA minimum for all ground beef, fresh or frozen. Use an instant-read thermometer through the side of the patty.

Can I cook a medium-rare burger?

Ground beef must reach 160°F / 71°C under USDA rules, which rules out rare and medium-rare. A steak can be cooked lower because its center never touched the surface; ground beef has no such exception.

Do burgers smoke in an air fryer?

Some smoke is normal with fatty beef. Keep the temperature at or below 380°F / 193°C, wipe the drawer between batches, and add a spoon of water to the drip tray.

Can I cook burgers and fries together?

Not well. Fries need shaking and dry air; burgers release fat. Cook the burgers first, wipe the drawer, then cook fries at 400°F / 205°C.

Burgers in the air fryer are simple once you accept the thermometer. Even patties with a dimple, 380°F / 193°C, a flip at halfway, and 160°F / 71°C in the middle — everything else is garnish.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
