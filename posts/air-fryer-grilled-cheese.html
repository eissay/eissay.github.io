Air Fryer Grilled Cheese: Time, Temperature and Crisp Rules

How to make grilled cheese in an air fryer, with 370°F / 188°C times for different breads, the flip rule and fixes for soggy or burnt results.

Grilled cheese is the fastest way to learn your air fryer's personality. There is no thermometer to check and no safe temperature to hit — just bread, butter and cheese, and a machine that browns the outside far faster than a skillet ever could.

The challenge is the opposite of every meat recipe: instead of worrying about the center, you are managing the surface. The air fryer will happily turn butter into gold and then into char in about two minutes, so the times below are about watching, not walking away.

Times vary by appliance model. Bread thickness, cheese moisture and butter coverage change the clock — there is no internal temperature target for this one.

Air fryer grilled cheese chart#

Assemble the sandwich with butter on the outside of both slices, then toast at a moderate temperature so the cheese melts before the bread darkens.

SandwichTemperatureTimeNotes
Standard white or sourdough370°F / 188°C8–10 minFlip at 4–5 min
Thick-cut artisan bread370°F / 188°C10–12 minFlip once, press lightly
Thin sandwich bread350°F / 177°C6–8 minWatch closely, flip halfway
Extra cheese, double decker350°F / 177°C10–12 minLonger melt, lower heat
Open-faced melt370°F / 188°C6–8 minNo flip, cheese on top

The flip is what separates even toasting from one golden side and one pale one. Use a spatula and flip confidently — melted cheese pulls, but a quick turn keeps the sandwich together.

Why 370°F / 188°C is the sweet spot#

Cheese melts fully between 150°F / 66°C and 180°F / 82°C, but it needs time. Bread browns quickly in moving hot air. A moderate temperature gives the cheese time to become pourable while the butter on the outside crisps gradually.

TemperatureWhat happens
320°F / 160°CCheese melts well, bread toasts slowly and evenly
350°F / 177°CGood balance for thick bread or heavy cheese
370°F / 188°CThe all-rounder: crisp outside, fully melted middle
400°F / 205°CFast, but the outside can char before the middle melts

If your first sandwich comes out dark on the outside and stiff in the middle, drop 20 degrees and add two minutes. The bread you use shifts the balance more than anything else — dense sourdough absorbs time, thin white bread burns in it.

The butter question#

Butter on the outside is the classic for a reason: it browns evenly and carries flavor into the crust. Two alternatives work in the air fryer:

  • Mayonnaise on the outside browns slightly faster and more evenly — a useful trick for breads that scorch.
  • Brushing the bread with oil works but browns patchy; butter and mayo coat more uniformly.

Butter the bread, not the basket. Butter in the basket smokes, spatters and makes cleanup miserable for no benefit — the sandwich carries its own fat.

Fillings that behave and fillings that fight#

The air fryer heats fillings with the same air that toasts the bread, so choose accordingly:

  • Slices of cheese melt evenly; shredded cheese melts faster but can slide out when flipped.
  • Thin tomato slices add moisture that softens the bread — pat them dry first.
  • Deli meats warm through in the same time; no adjustment needed.
  • Thick or cold fillings (chili, pulled meat) insulate the middle — lower the temperature and extend the time so the heat reaches the center.

Two slices of cheese is the reliability limit. Beyond that, the outer bread browns before the stack melts, and the fix is always lower-and-longer, not hotter.

Five mistakes with air fryer grilled cheese#

  1. Starting at 400°F / 205°C. The crust wins the race and the cheese stays firm in the middle.
  2. Skipping the flip. One-sided toasting is the most common complaint and the easiest fix.
  3. Buttering the basket instead of the bread. All smoke, no crunch.
  4. Piling on four slices of cheese. The stack insulates; lower the heat or accept unmelted pockets.
  5. Walking away. The difference between golden and burnt is two minutes in moving air.

Frequently asked questions#

What temperature do you grill cheese in an air fryer?

370°F / 188°C for standard bread, 8–10 minutes with a flip halfway. Use 350°F / 177°C for thick bread, extra cheese or delicate fillings.

How do you keep the sandwich from flying around?

Lighter baskets can lift thin sandwiches. A metal toast rack or two toothpicks through the top corners holds it down — remove them before cutting.

Do you need to preheat for grilled cheese?

Two or three minutes helps the first sandwich brown evenly. It is less critical than for meat, since there is no safety timing involved.

Why is my bread burnt but the cheese cold?

The temperature was too high or the fillings too thick. Drop to 350°F / 177°C, extend the time, and let cheese slices sit at room temperature for ten minutes before assembling.

Can you reheat a grilled cheese in the air fryer?

Yes — 350°F / 177°C for 3–4 minutes brings back most of the crunch. Add a minute for thick or double-decker sandwiches.

Grilled cheese teaches the core air fryer skill: controlling surface browning with temperature and time. Master the flip and the 370°F / 188°C sweet spot, and every melt, toast and quesadilla that follows will behave.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
