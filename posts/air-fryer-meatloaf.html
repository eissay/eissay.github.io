Air Fryer Meatloaf: Time and Temperature Chart

Air fryer meatloaf times by loaf size, with glaze timing, mini meatloaf settings and the internal temperature ground meat must reach.

Meatloaf is the classic oven dish the air fryer quietly does better: the circulating heat browns the whole surface, the glaze sets instead of steaming, and a weeknight loaf is done in half the time. The catch is that a loaf is dense, and dense ground meat is the easiest thing in the kitchen to undercook in the middle.

This chart covers loaf sizes and glaze timing. Like all ground meat under USDA rules, meatloaf must reach 160°F / 71°C inside — the glaze, the crust and the size of the loaf do not change that.

Times vary by appliance model — use a thermometer. Wattage, basket size and loaf shape change the clock, but 160°F / 71°C does not change.

Air fryer meatloaf chart#

Roast meatloaf at a moderate temperature so the center cooks through before the outside hardens. Rest the loaf 5 minutes after cooking so it slices cleanly.

The probe target is the geometric center of the loaf. Insert the thermometer from the side or the end, horizontally, so the tip sits in the middle of the thickest part. When it reads 160°F / 71°C, the loaf is done everywhere.

Why the air fryer suits meatloaf#

An oven heats meatloaf from all sides at once, but the air around the loaf is still — the crust forms slowly and the glaze often steams. The air fryer moves hot air across every surface, so the Maillard browning arrives early and the glaze caramelizes instead of softening.

StageWhat happens
First 15 minutesFat renders into the drip tray; surface begins to brown
Mid-cookGlaze sets; edges pull slightly from the pan
Final minutesThermometer confirms the center; glaze darkens
Rest, 5 minutesJuices settle; slices hold together

Because the air fryer browns faster than an oven, a sugary glaze can go from mahogany to burnt in minutes. Apply half the glaze in the last 10 minutes and the rest after the loaf is out — you get color without char.

Bread crumbs, milk and the binder#

The binder is what keeps meatloaf tender instead of dense. Bread crumbs soaked in milk (or any liquid) hold moisture as the loaf cooks and create the soft crumb that defines the dish. A standard ratio is one egg and a quarter cup of liquid-soaked crumbs per pound of meat.

Two practical notes for the air fryer specifically:

  • Loaf pans fit most baskets but block air underneath; free-form loaves cook more evenly.
  • A pan also catches fat. If your basket has no drip clearance, place the loaf on a small rack or a few onion slices so grease drains away from the meat.

Five mistakes with air fryer meatloaf#

  1. Trusting the crust color. The outside browns long before the center is done; only a center probe counts.
  2. Cooking at 400°F / 205°C. High heat sets the crust early and leaves the middle behind.
  3. Skipping the rest. Cutting straight from the basket spills the juices and crumbles the slice.
  4. Glazing from the start. Sugary glazes scorch; glaze late in thin layers.
  5. Packing the meat too tight. Compressed meat shrinks hard and cooks unevenly; shape it loosely.

Frequently asked questions#

How long does meatloaf take in an air fryer?

25–35 minutes at 350°F / 177°C for a 1 pound loaf, 35–45 minutes for 1.5 pounds, and 45–55 minutes for 2 pounds. Mini meatloaves take 15–20 minutes at 375°F / 190°C.

What temperature should meatloaf be inside?

160°F / 71°C in the center. Meatloaf is ground meat, and USDA rules require that internal temperature regardless of loaf size.

Can I use a loaf pan in the air fryer?

Yes, if it fits with air space around it. Metal and silicone pans both work; expect a few extra minutes versus free-form, since the pan shields the bottom.

Why is my meatloaf dry?

Too little binder, too long a cook, or very lean meat. Soak the crumbs, verify the center promptly at 160°F / 71°C, and rest the loaf before slicing.

Can I cook meatloaf from frozen?

Raw meatloaf should be thawed before cooking — the center must reach 160°F / 71°C, and a frozen loaf browns outside while the middle is still thawing. Cooked slices and reheating leftovers to steaming work fine in the basket.

Meatloaf rewards moderation: moderate heat, a verified center, a rest before slicing. Get the middle to 160°F / 71°C and the air fryer will hand you the best crust this dish has ever had.

Always verify doneness with an instant-read thermometer. Cooking times are starting points and vary by appliance model, wattage and basket size.
